HandSpace

Webcam hand + head tracking in a 3D room.

Pinch your thumb and index finger near a shape to pick it up. Move your hand closer or further from the camera to reach deeper into the room. Move your head to look around the room, as if the screen were a window.

All tracking runs locally in your browser. No video leaves your machine.
