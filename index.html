HandSpace

Webcam hand + head tracking in a 3D room.

Playground: pinch your thumb and index finger near a shape to pick it up. Move your hand closer or further from the camera to reach deeper into the room.

Boxing: punch towards the screen to hit the heavy bag and the yellow mitt.

Move your head to look around the room, as if the screen were a window. Switch modes with the tabs at the top.

All tracking runs locally in your browser. No video leaves your machine.
